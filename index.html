Privacy Policy

Abyss Angler · Paquett Studio · Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Abyss Angler (com.paquettstudio.abyssangler), published by Paquett Studio. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version: Abyss Angler itself does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no accounts and no analytics. The App Store (iOS) version can show optional reward adverts; the advertising partner that serves them processes some technical device information, described in section 4. The Google Play version currently contains no adverts and makes no network requests at all.

1. Information we collect

We collect none. We do not ask for, store on our servers, or transmit any information about you. The game has no accounts and never requests:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any credentials;
  • your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files;
  • analytics, usage statistics or diagnostic telemetry sent to us.

On iOS, when the game loads or shows an advert, the advertising partner receives some technical information directly from your device — see section 4. That data goes to them, not to us; we never see it and could not identify you from it.

On Google Play, the current version contains no advertising software and makes no network requests. It is fully playable offline; so is the iOS version, apart from the adverts themselves.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, using your device's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is never readable by us. It consists only of:

What is savedExamples
Your progress Level, experience, coins, pearls, materials, upgrades, research, vessel
Your collection Which fish you have caught and your best catch of each species
Your choices Captain name, chosen portrait, equipped title, sound and graphics settings
Daily state Quest progress, streak counter, spins remaining, tutorial position

The captain name is text you type yourself. It is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is never shown to any other player. If you type your real name, it stays as private as anything else on your phone.

3. Deleting your data

Because your save is stored only on your device, deleting it is entirely in your hands:

  • Android: uninstall Abyss Angler, or open Settings › Apps › Abyss Angler › Storage and choose Clear data;
  • iOS: delete the app from the Home Screen (touch and hold › Remove App › Delete App).

Either action removes your save permanently. It cannot be recovered, by you or by us. For data processed by the advertising partner, see their policy linked in section 4.

4. Third parties and advertising

Google Play version: contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no social media plugins and no third-party software development kits that collect data. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is gathered.

App Store (iOS) version: shows optional reward adverts — short videos you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. They are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity company) and the advertising networks it mediates. You are never required to watch one; every advert in the game sits behind a clearly labelled button. To keep an advert ready, the game contacts the advertising service in the background while you play.

When an advert is loaded or shown, the advertising partner processes information such as:

  • your IP address, and the approximate region derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version and language;
  • a device advertising identifier, where available (see below);
  • advert events — which advert was shown, whether it was watched to the end.

This is used to serve and measure adverts and to prevent fraud. It is their processing, under their policy: see Unity's privacy policy.

App Tracking Transparency: the current iOS version does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps. Without that permission, iOS withholds the advertising identifier from the partner, and adverts are served without it.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's other data services (Analytics, Crash Reporting and In-App Purchasing) are disabled in both versions.

5. Payments

The current version of Abyss Angler contains no in-app purchases and no way to spend real money. All currencies in the game are earned by playing. Watching a reward advert never costs anything.

6. Children

Abyss Angler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. The advertising configuration declares the game as not directed to children, and the game contains no chat, no social features and no way to share personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future update introduces in-app purchases, advertising on further platforms, or any other feature that involves data, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be described in the update notes on the relevant store. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to paquettstudio@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Abyss Angler is developed and published by Paquett Studio.
This policy applies to the Google Play and App Store releases of the game and to no other product.
