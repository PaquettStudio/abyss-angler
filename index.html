Privacy Policy

Abyss Angler · Paquett Studio · Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Abyss Angler (com.paquettstudio.abyssangler), published by Paquett Studio. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version: we never ask for your email, your name, your location or your contacts. The game sends us anonymous usage counts — things like “someone reached 500 m” — tied to a random number generated on your device and to nothing else, and you can switch them off — see section 1. An account is created only if you tap “Keep a copy of my progress”, and you can delete it from inside the game whenever you like. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google — we never see your card. Both versions can show optional reward adverts; the partner that serves them processes some technical device information, and they are personalised only if you say yes when the game asks you — both described in section 4.

1. Information we collect

We never ask for your email address, phone number, password, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The game requests none of those permissions and has no way to read any of it. There is no advertising profile of you, and we sell nothing to anyone.

Two things do leave your device. One happens by default and is anonymous; the other happens only if you ask for it.

Anonymous usage counts

To know which parts of the game work and where people get stuck, the game sends us small counted events — for example “a player finished the tutorial”, “a player reached 500 m”, “a reward advert was watched”. Each one carries the name of the event, the time, sometimes a number (a depth, a level), the game version and whether the device is iOS or Android.

They are grouped under a random identifier created on your device the first time you play. It is not your advertising ID, not your device serial number, and not linked to your name, your account or your purchases. We cannot work backwards from it to a person, and it disappears when you uninstall the game or delete your account.

You can turn this off. Open Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics in the game and switch it to OFF. Nothing is sent from that moment, and anything waiting to be sent is discarded. The game plays exactly the same either way, and we do not ask again.

If your copy of the game does not show that switch yet, it arrives in the next update. Uninstalling the game also stops the counts at once and takes the identifier with it, on any version.

An account

An account exists so that your progress survives a lost or replaced phone. It is created at the moment you tap “Keep a copy of my progress” on the Account screen — not at first launch, not in the background, not as a condition of playing. If you never tap it, no account is ever created and nothing about you is stored anywhere but your own device.

When you do create one, the account holds:

  • a random player identifier we generate (for example p_4f9c…);
  • a one-way hash of a random key your device creates the first time it registers. It is not your device's advertising ID or serial number, we cannot reverse it, and it identifies the installation rather than you;
  • a friend code — eight characters, generated by us, that you can give to people;
  • your captain name, if you have set one, and the portrait you chose;
  • a small summary of your play: level, deepest dive, how many species you have logged, how many achievements you have earned, your current vessel and title, and catch counts;
  • the date of your last visit.

If you give your friend code to someone and they add you, they can see your captain name, your portrait and that summary of play. Nobody else can, and there is no public list of players.

If you also turn on progress backup, a copy of your save (the same data described in section 2) is stored with the account so that a new phone can pick it up.

When the game loads or shows an advert, the advertising partner receives some technical information directly from your device — see section 4. That data goes to them, not to us; we never see it and could not identify you from it.

Both versions are playable offline. The game contacts our server for two things only: the usage counts, if you have left them on, and your account, if you created one. With the counts off and no account, it never contacts us at all.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, using your device's standard app storage. Unless you have created an account and turned on backup, this data never leaves your device and is never readable by us. It consists only of:

What is savedExamples
Your progress Level, experience, coins, pearls, materials, upgrades, research, vessel
Your collection Which fish you have caught and your best catch of each species
Your choices Captain name, chosen portrait, equipped title, sound and graphics settings
Daily state Quest progress, streak counter, Deep Scans remaining, tutorial position

The captain name is text you type yourself. If you have no account it stays on your device. If you have one, it is stored with the account and shown to people you add as friends — so it is the one field worth choosing with that in mind. If you type your real name, it stays as private as anything else on your phone until you create an account.

3. Deleting your data

On your device:

  • Android: uninstall Abyss Angler, or open Settings › Apps › Abyss Angler › Storage and choose Clear data;
  • iOS: delete the app from the Home Screen (touch and hold › Remove App › Delete App).

On our server, if you created an account: open the Account screen in the game and choose “Delete this account”. It asks twice, and then removes the account, the backup copy of your save, your friend code and every friendship attached to it. Nothing is kept, and it cannot be undone by you or by us.

Deleting the app alone does not delete the account, because the account is the thing that was meant to outlive the app. Use the in-game button for that.

For data processed by the advertising partner, see their policy linked in section 4. For purchases, see section 5 — Apple and Google keep their own records of what you paid, and we cannot delete those.

4. Third parties and advertising

Apart from the advertising partner described below, no third-party tracking software is built into either version. There is no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no attribution or install-tracking service and no social media plugin. The usage counts in section 1 are our own, they go to our own server, and nobody else receives them.

Both versions (from version 2.0 on Google Play) show optional reward adverts — short videos you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. They are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity company) and the advertising networks it mediates. You are never required to watch one; every advert in the game sits behind a clearly labelled button. To keep an advert ready, the game contacts the advertising service in the background while you play.

When an advert is loaded or shown, the advertising partner processes information such as:

  • your IP address, and the approximate region derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version and language;
  • a device advertising identifier, where available (see below);
  • advert events — which advert was shown, whether it was watched to the end.

This is used to serve and measure adverts and to prevent fraud. It is their processing, under their policy: see Unity's privacy policy.

Personalised adverts are your choice. The first time you play, the game asks you once whether adverts may be personalised, with two equal buttons, Yes and No. No advert is requested before you answer. Your answer is passed to our partner before it starts: No means we tell it that you have not given consent under the GDPR and that your information must not be sold or shared under the CCPA, so adverts are chosen without a profile of you. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy › Personalised adverts. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone’s own settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads on some phones).

App Tracking Transparency (iOS): on iOS, personalisation also needs Apple’s permission. If you answer Yes to the game’s question, Apple’s own Tracking prompt follows — Apple shows that question, not us. If you answer No, the game does not show Apple’s prompt at all, and iOS withholds the advertising identifier from our partner.

Saying no changes nothing about the game. You get the same features, the same rewards and the same number of adverts; they are simply chosen without knowing anything about you. You can change your answer at any time in the game’s Settings, and on iOS also in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Your answer is kept on your phone; it is not sent to us.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Analytics and Unity Crash Reporting are disabled in both versions. Unity's In-App Purchasing component is used to talk to the Apple and Google stores — see section 6.

5. Our server

The account features in section 1 and the usage counts both run on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database, acting as our processor. Requests reach it over HTTPS. Servers are located in the United States and the European Union, and Cloudflare transfers data under the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

We do not store your IP address in our database. Cloudflare sees it in order to deliver the request, as any web host does, and keeps its own short-lived logs. From it we keep one thing: the two-letter country code of an account, so we know which regions the game is played in. Not the city, not the address, not the coordinates.

The usage counts are stored separately from accounts, under the random identifier described in section 1. Nothing joins the two tables — there is no column that could.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. It exists for two purposes: giving you your progress back, and telling us which parts of the game to fix.

6. Payments

Abyss Angler offers optional in-app purchases: packs of Black Pearls, a Starter Kit, a season pass and a monthly VIP subscription. Everything they contain can also be earned by playing; they buy time, not access. Where a version of the game does not offer them, the store simply shows them as unavailable and nothing changes for you.

We never see your payment details. The purchase is made with Apple or Google, using the payment method already on your store account. They take the money, they issue the receipt, and they tell the game only that a specific product was bought. No card number, billing address or bank detail ever reaches us or is stored by us.

The VIP subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. You cancel it in your own store account — Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iOS — and cancelling is as easy as subscribing. Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, not by us.

Watching a reward advert never costs anything, and nothing in the game requires a purchase.

7. Children

Abyss Angler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child. We ask for no age, no email and no name beyond a captain nickname you type yourself, so we have no way to identify a child — and we would rather keep it that way than build one. The advertising configuration declares the game as not directed to children.

The game contains no chat and no way to send another player free text. The only social feature is a friend code you choose to share, which lets the people you add see your captain name, portrait and a summary of your play. Gifts between friends are switched off in the current version.

If you believe a child has created an account in the game, write to the address in section 9 and we will delete it. No proof of identity will be demanded for a deletion request.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy is updated before a change reaches you, not after, and the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes are also described in the update notes on the relevant store.

Features we are building, and which are not in the game today:

  • Signing in with Game Center or Google Play Games, so your progress follows your store account instead of a transfer code. If it ships, we will store a one-way hash of the identifier those services give us — never your email address and never your real name.
  • Gifts between friends — small fixed in-game gifts, no free text.

None of these is active yet. Each one will appear on this page before it appears in the game.
