Privacy Policy

Abyss Angler · Paquett Studio · Last updated 9 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Abyss Angler (com.paquettstudio.abyssangler), published by Paquett Studio. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version: Abyss Angler does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no internet connection of any kind. Everything it remembers is stored on your own device and disappears when you uninstall it.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, request, store on our servers, or transmit any information about you or your device. Specifically, the game does not ask for or access:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials;
  • your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files;
  • your advertising identifier, device identifier or IP address;
  • analytics, crash reports, usage statistics or diagnostic telemetry.

Abyss Angler requests no Android permissions beyond those every app receives by default, and it makes no network requests. It is fully playable with the device offline or in aeroplane mode.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, using Android's standard app-storage mechanism. This data never leaves your device and is never readable by us. It consists only of:

What is savedExamples
Your progress Level, experience, coins, pearls, materials, upgrades, research, vessel
Your collection Which fish you have caught and your best catch of each species
Your choices Captain name, chosen portrait, equipped title, sound and graphics settings
Daily state Quest progress, streak counter, spins remaining, tutorial position

The captain name is text you type yourself. It is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is never shown to any other player. If you type your real name, it stays as private as anything else on your phone.

3. Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored off your device, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request for us to process. To erase everything the game has saved, either:

  • uninstall Abyss Angler; or
  • open Settings › Apps › Abyss Angler › Storage and choose Clear data.

Either action removes your save permanently. It cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

4. Third parties

Abyss Angler contains no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no social media plugins and no third-party software development kits that collect data. No information is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone, because none is gathered in the first place.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's own data-collection services (Analytics, Ads, Crash Reporting and In-App Purchasing) are disabled in this build.

5. Payments

The current version of Abyss Angler contains no in-app purchases and no way to spend real money. All currencies in the game are earned by playing.

6. Children

Abyss Angler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. Since the game collects nothing and connects to nothing, it presents no mechanism by which a child's data could be gathered.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future update introduces advertising, in-app purchases or any other feature that involves data, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be described in the update notes on Google Play. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to paquettstudio@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Abyss Angler is developed and published by Paquett Studio.
This policy applies to the Google Play release of the game and to no other product.
